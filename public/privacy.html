Beezy Luxury Detailing

Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026 · Beta

Beezy Luxury Detailing is a mobile auto detailing business serving the Greater New Orleans area. This policy explains what the Beezy app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version. We collect what we need to wash your car at your address and bill you for it: who you are, where you are, what you drive, and photos of it. We do not sell your data, we run no advertising or analytics trackers, and we never see your card number. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Name, email, phoneTo identify your account, confirm bookings, send receipts, and tell you when Beezy is on the way.
Service address and map locationThis is a mobile service. We need to know where your car is, and we use it to plan the day's route so arrival times are honest.
Vehicle details — year, make, model, colour, optional VIN and platePrice and time depend on the size of the vehicle. A VIN, if you give one, is decoded to fill in the rest for you.

Photo timestamp and locationAttached to before/after photos, to establish when and where the work was done.
Payment recordsAmounts, dates, and a token that identifies your card to our payment processor.

What we never collect

  • Your card number. Card details go directly to Square and never touch our systems. We store only a token that lets Square charge the card you approved.
  • Your location in the background. We ask for location only while you are booking, to confirm your address.
  • Advertising identifiers. There is no advertising SDK and no analytics tracker in this app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Your photos and the public gallery

Photos of your vehicle appear publicly only if you explicitly consent. Consent is asked for separately at booking, it is off by default, and you can withdraw it at any time — we will remove the photos from the gallery when you do.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: More → Profile → Delete account. You do not need to email anyone. You can also request deletion at the address below.

When you delete your account we remove:

  • Your name, email, phone number, and addresses
  • Your vehicles and their details
  • Your photos, including any in the public gallery
  • Your notes and preferences
One thing we keep. Records of services you actually paid for — the date, the amount, and what was done — are kept in anonymised form for seven years, because a business has legal and tax obligations on transactions that really happened. These records are stripped of your name, contact details and address, and are no longer linked to you.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA — including the right to object to processing and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Email us and we will help; we aim to respond within 30 days.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is enforced per-record at the database level, so one customer's account cannot read another's. Card data is handled entirely by Square, which is PCI-DSS compliant.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
